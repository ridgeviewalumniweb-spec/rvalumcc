RACC Recovery Walk

Sponsored by the Ridgeview Alumni Association, the annual Recovery Walk is a morning on the trails with alumni, families, friends, and plenty of four-legged supporters, celebrating recovery out loud.

Picnic · every spring

Spring Fling

The Ridgeview Alumni Association's annual picnic: burgers and hot dogs off the grill, music, face painting, the pie-eating contest, and the much-anticipated raffle. Raffle proceeds go to RACC. In 2026 the Fling raised $2,500 for scholarships.
