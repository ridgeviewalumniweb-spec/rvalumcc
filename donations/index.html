Why give?

Your gift keeps recovery from stopping short

When you donate to the Ridgeview Alumni Charitable Corporation, you give someone the opportunity to turn their life around. Your generosity has given many people the chance to receive treatment for substance use and mental health disorders at the Partial Hospitalization or Intensive Outpatient levels of care.

  • More time in treatment. PHP and IOP care when insurance stops paying.
  • A safe place to stay. Housing during treatment, without the isolation, the long commute, or a triggering home environment.
  • Medication. The price of medications or co-pays can be prohibitive in early recovery. Your kindness removes the temptation to think, "I can do without it."
