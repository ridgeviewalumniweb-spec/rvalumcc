What your gift does

Support through every stage of early recovery

Scholarships go directly toward care, so no one has to stop short because the money ran out.

More time in treatment

Funding for Partial Hospitalization (PHP) and Intensive Outpatient (IOP) care when insurance stops paying.

A safe place to stay

Housing during treatment and help with sober living, away from isolation, long commutes, or a triggering home.

Medication

Help with prescriptions and co-pays, so cost is never the reason someone goes without.

Education

Educational support that helps people build the skills and stability for lasting recovery.

About us

Remember the people who lifted you up?

Remember hitting your bottom? Do you remember that moment when you first began to feel some hope?

Looking back, can you remember those angels who appeared at that precise moment when you needed help the most? I can. They helped lift me up until I could stand on my own.

The Ridgeview Alumni Charitable Corporation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, was established to financially help people who need treatment for substance use disorders and mental health challenges. Whether it's more time in treatment, housing, medications, or educational support, a helping hand at that critical moment can make all the difference in the world.

Our mission

To provide support through education, treatment, medication, and housing to individuals in need of mental health and substance abuse services.

Our vision

We believe that substance use disorders and mental health challenges can be treated effectively. We envision a future where everyone facing these issues is treated with compassion and respect, and given the chance to make positive changes in their lives and become productive members of their community.

That opportunity should be there for anyone who needs it, wants it, and is willing to take the necessary action.
